# Designcraft's expertise

One brief per area of the craft: what a master knows that a beginner doesn't. The lead reads a brief when a job reaches that area, or hands it to a helper working there.

A good brief:

- Names the area in a few words and says when to read it.
- Teaches the fundamentals as working knowledge: what good looks like, the common ways it goes wrong, and how a master fixes each one.
- Gives examples, ideally from real work Sean has seen or made.
- Stays craft, not procedure. No steps to follow, no sign-offs, no rules about other rules.
- Fits in about 1,500 words. When it grows past that, split it.

Book notes stay in the studio's private library, which git ignores. A brief here is written in the studio's own words and never copies book text.

## The briefs

In the order a design usually meets them:

- **Layout and what the eye reads first.** Starting from the content, ranking what matters, space that groups, grids, and long scrolling stories.
- **Type.** Choosing and pairing faces, a scale of sizes, line length and spacing, display type that carries a brand, and numbers in tables.
- **Colour.** Palettes with enough shades, colour that carries meaning without being the only signal, contrast, and dark mode as its own design.
- **A design system, and turning a brand on its head.** What carries a brand, so everything else can be broken without losing it.
- **How a screen behaves.** Every state a screen has, feedback for every action, forms that forgive, and undo over confirm.
- **Every screen size and everyone.** Re-composing for each screen, touch, and access for people who see poorly or don't use a mouse.
- **Motion in an interface.** What motion is for, how fast, scroll that tells a story, and the line where Motioncraft takes over.
- **Presentations.** The story skeleton from Sean's practice deck, then the look: clear and a spectacle at once.

Each brief closes with "What a strong designer would notice": a short list of the things a master spots at once. The lead uses it for the teaching part of a review page.
