# Spotify Wrapped 2024, the year that was panned, and the 2025 fix

## What it is

Wrapped is the recap Spotify shows each listener every December, inside its music app. It tells your year of listening as a run of full-screen cards you tap through, like stories on a phone. Each card carries one number or one name. Spotify says it began the idea in 2015.

This entry is a caution, in two parts. The 2024 edition (December 4, 2024) had a sound structure and loud visuals, and listeners still called it a letdown. The 2025 edition (December 3, 2025) is what Spotify changed in answer.

See it: https://newsroom.spotify.com/2025-12-03/2025-wrapped-user-experience/

That is Spotify's own walk through the 2025 cards. Wrapped itself only shows inside the app, with your own data.

**Best moment: the top-song quiz in 2025, about 45 seconds in.** Before the card names your most-played song, it shows a short list and asks you to guess. You commit, then it reveals. Compare it with the three "Music Evolution" cards in 2024, about 1 minute 40 in. Each names a month and gives it an invented genre label several words long. TechCrunch quoted one listener calling those cards strange.

## How it turned out

2024, the caution:

- **Press on launch day.** TechCrunch's headline called it underwhelming. It reported that favourite parts were gone, top genres above all, while an AI-made podcast about your year had been added. It said views on the artwork were mixed and that several users called it simple.
- **Measured mood.** CARMA, a media-monitoring firm, found that positive talk about Spotify fell from 50.5% to 41.5% after the release, and negative talk rose from 8.3% to 13.6%. That is one firm's measure, reported by Marketing-Interactive.
- **Still huge.** Fortune reports that 245 million people opened Wrapped in 2024. Panned is not the same as ignored.

2025, the fix:

- **Spotify reversed the cuts.** Fortune reports that top genres came back and AI shrank to a single feature. Spotify's marketing chief said the aim was a Wrapped rooted in "human creativity and connection".
- **Faster uptake.** By Spotify's own count, 200 million people opened it in about 24 hours. In 2024 that took about 62 hours. Shares rose 41% to about 500 million.

## What makes it good, measured

Wrapped cannot be replayed, so I measured from screen recordings that three listeners posted on YouTube: two of 2024 and one of 2025. I took one frame every 3 seconds and read the cards by eye. Each listener gets slightly different cards, so treat the counts as typical, not exact.

2024:

- **Length:** about 3 minutes 30 seconds from the greeting to the final summary card. The two recordings ran 3:37 and 3:50 in all.
- **About 14 cards, about 15 seconds each.** In order: greeting, minutes listened, biggest listening day, songs played, top song, top five songs, a lead-in line, three Music Evolution cards, artists played, top artist, top five artists, sign-off. Then a summary card made for sharing.
- **Words per card:** one sentence of 5 to 10 words, then a second, smaller line that fades in a few seconds later.
- **What you can touch:** tap forward, tap back, pause, share. Nothing else.
- **Look:** a black ground with flat, fully saturated colour, one colour per chapter: hot pink, electric blue, yellow near #f8f808, red near #e81808, bright green. Most cards sit in the same stepped zigzag frame, like oversized pixels. The greeting and the three Music Evolution cards use ribbon shapes on a dark ground. Type is one centred sans, white on most cards and black on the yellow ones.

2025:

- **Length:** about 3 minutes to the summary card, in about 10 chapters. An optional archive of longer written reports follows.
- **What came back:** top genres.
- **What you can touch:** more. The top-song quiz, a month-by-month race between your top artists, and a "club" you are sorted into.
- **Look:** close to black and white. An off-white near #e8e8e8 and a charcoal near #282828, with stripes, dots and hand-drawn scribbles. One accent colour appears at a time: a lime near #98c808 for the "listening age" card, a red-orange near #f84838 for the club. Headlines are a wide, heavy sans. Spotify called the look a "visual mixtape".

What was bent and what was kept, both years: the app's everyday look, dark screens with one green, gives way to a new palette each December. The small round logo stays at the top of every card, and so does the format of one stat per card.

Against Sean's four story rules, for 2024:

- **Every headline states its point: kept.** The headline is the stat.
- **Plant something early that the close pays off: broken.** My read: the opening line sets up nothing. The one new idea, Music Evolution, gets a lead-in card, and its payoff is a label listeners did not recognise as theirs.
- **One idea per slide: kept.** One per card, every card.
- **The reveal happens in silence: half kept.** Each stat lands in two beats, headline then second line. But the listener never guesses or waits, so nothing is at stake before the reveal. The 2025 quiz fixes exactly that.

### Why it's here

It is the shelf's warning. Wrapped 2024 kept most of Sean's rules and looked loud, and people still felt short-changed. My read: structure and colour cannot stand in for the thing the audience came for, and one frame repeated on most cards stops reading as spectacle by the fourth. The 2025 answer is the useful part: give back the substance, strip the colour down, and let the audience take part in the reveal.
