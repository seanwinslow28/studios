# Devcraft's shelf of good work

Real examples of excellent small software: tools, fixes and scripts that are well built, well tested and easy to undo, each vetted for how it turned out: who made it, how it was received, and what makes it good, measured rather than admired. Step 2 of every job starts here, then adds the examples that job needs.

One file per example or small group, holding:

- What it is, who made it, when, and a link.
- How it turned out: how it was received, and why that's believable.
- What makes it good, measured: the numbers, structure and choices behind the effect.
- What Sean said about it, in his words, if he has seen it.

Links and words only. Other people's work never goes in this repo or in a finished piece.

## Where the shelf stands

Sean saw all 8 pieces on November 10, 2026, and picked uv's "upgrade without breaking" design as closest to what Devcraft should make: one small mechanism, nothing deleted that might still be in use, and honest notes on where it doesn't help. He didn't single out the others; ask about them when a job leans on one.
