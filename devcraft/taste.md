# Devcraft's taste

Sean's taste for small software: tools, fixes and scripts that are well built, well tested and easy to undo, in his own words: the things he caught and wants caught next time. The lead reads this page at the start of every job, and the front door's brainstorm bends ideas through it.

How this page works:

- Each rule is one line in Sean's words, with the example he caught and when.
- About 15 rules at most. Only Sean raises that ceiling.
- A rule joins when Sean has given the same note about five times and says yes to the lead's proposal, or when he writes one himself.
- Rules come from taste, never from something that went wrong once. A mistake gets fixed in the work.

## Rules

Written with Sean on November 10, 2026, from his rulings on the old Devcraft jobs and his pick from the shelf.

1. **One small mechanism, nothing deleted that might still be in use, and honest notes on where it stops.** His pick of uv's "upgrade without breaking" design as the model for what Devcraft makes (November 10).
2. **No endless fix loops: "the train cannot keep going back and forth in an open-ended loop of small fixes."** A job settles or stops. He said it when he stopped the old shadow-guard job (October 13).
3. **Show him the result working, never the agent's steps to grade.** "There's absolutely no way for me to do all 34 of these" (October 21).
4. **Built for how he actually uses it: safe for ordinary use, never made so safe it's useless.** He turned down a guard rule that would have dropped most of his real traffic (October 12), and accepted that the guard didn't need to stop deliberate tricks (October 13).
5. **Fix the real gaps found along the way, within the budget, rather than writing them down as known limits.** He overrode the lead's pick on this in the old guard-fix job (October 13).
6. **Putting it in and switching it on are two separate yeses.** He approved the guard "merged and off", then gave the go-live yes the next day (October 13 and 14).
7. **Before anything goes live, tell him plainly what it touches and what undoing it really undoes.** His go-live yes came after being told exactly that (October 14).
