# Motioncraft's front-door lens

What a film needs from the brief room, read by the shared spine ([`/front-door`](../../.claude/skills/front-door/SKILL.md)) when Sean runs `/front-door motioncraft <the idea>`. The spine owns the procedure; this folder owns only the film: the ten points the grill must nail, the two nevers, the look stage's hosts and forms, the brief lines the studio's gates will attack, and the toolbelt. The first train (mc-eng-001) did everything its brief asked and its brief carried eleven ASK lines and one sentence of look; what never crossed was *what good looks like*, in a form the Director's intake and Art's packet could be held to. This lens is where that crosses. ## At the start

Before `[L1]`:

1. **The media folder** is mapped (`folders.local`, `media =`); the piece's folder and `Planning/<slug>/` are made there. The ledger is not touched: the room writes nothing under `motioncraft/ledger/`.
2. **The Higgsfield meter.** `higgsfield account status` for the balance; Sean names the session's ceiling in credits, locked as `[L2] CEILING`. It is the only ceiling: stills on ChatGPT desktop and the Codex image path run on the subscription. Every motion round says `spent / ceiling`; the next clip that would cross it stops the rounds.
3. **The settings**, as a checked absence: ChatGPT's "Improve the model for everyone" off and Codex's "Include environments" unchecked (mc-setup-001), before any image is generated on either host. The room reaches no other cloud tool.
4. **The approvals ledger** is read for the pieces' precedents only when the piece continues an approved one; a new piece starts from Sean's words, and the earlier film's rejected looks are read for what he named wrong, never as a look to match.

## The ten points: the film's North Star

The grill asks these in this order, which is the brief's order, one question each with a recommendation, and stops when every one is a named specific. Each line says what the Director's intake will map it to, so the room writes it in a form the intake can hold.

1. **The single objective.** The one thing this film must do. Three candidates means the grill is not done. Maps to the brief's opening breath.
2. **Who feels it.** The audience as a feeling, not a demographic. Shapes the register and the length.
3. **Where it lives, and what that fixes.** The channel, and from it the length, the ratio, the frame rate and the delivery files; the master skill's defaults (1920×1080, 24 fps, ProRes master and H.264 copy) unless the channel says otherwise. Maps to the constraints and the source contract.
4. **The references, with their what.** Each a board row (below): what is borrowed in one of the lens's words (timing, palette, staging, line, texture, shape language, motion), and what is not. A reference without a what is a category answer. Maps to *What good looks like*.
5. **The subjects.** Each thing or character in the film, with one design-bearing specific and what must stay recognisable through every transformation. Not a design: a handle Art can design from and a gate can check identity against. Maps to ASK lines and the appearance gate's identity checks.
6. **The hero moment and the signature move.** The one image the film builds toward, and the one move that is the film's, with its **timing feel in words a gate can hold**. Sean's plain words land verbatim; where the glossary has the term (anticipation, slow into the rest, smear, hold), the room writes it in brackets beside his words for the Director to carry, and never replaces his. Maps to ASK lines the story gate and motion gate read.
7. **Sound scope and every word.** "Texture and accents only", or the score, voice or dialogue and its source; every on-screen or spoken word as Sean's or a marked placeholder (`WP<n>`), never the room's. Maps to `sound_scope:` and the words rule.
8. **The constraints.** The engagement type the room recommends (full train, small piece, one-off), the machine, the budgets it knows (attention stops, the Higgsfield spend already made), the deadline or `none`, and every approved element that must be used unchanged, by its approvals id. Maps to the brief header and the parts list.
9. **The non-negotiables, as checkable directives.** Lines a reviewer could verdict a frame, a clip or a file against: frame counts, holds, volumes, continuity rules, what reads muted. Maps to ASK lines and the gates' attack lists.
10. **What this is NOT.** The nearest plausible wrong versions, fenced: the wrong medium, the wrong register, the wrong joke, the look Sean already rejected. Maps to the appearance gate's "The rejected look back" and the story gate's "The opening redesigned" kind of line.

## The two nevers

- **The room never designs the look.** It finds the direction with Sean's eye, on a board and a walkthrough, and hands Art a taste reference and a board; Art's appearance packet designs the look, and the appearance gate and Sean judge it. A chosen hero frame is never a style frame, never a frozen look, and never pasted into a packet as one (never-do 8).
- **The room never writes the beat sheet, the boards or the timing.** Those are the Director's story packet. The room may render **a few key story shots** as look-stage frames, the hero moment and two or three waypoints, so the look is judged on the film's own moments; the Director makes the animatic from his own boards, with those frames beside the brief as references, never as panels.

## The look stage

Three parts, in order, every one after the ten points are locked.

**1. The board, first.** [board-template.md](board-template.md): one row per piece of existing work, one table of negatives, thumbnails for Sean's eye only. Built before the first render, because the walkthrough's prompts draw their language from the board's borrowed-whats and from nothing invented in the room. A row cited on two pieces is offered for a standing library, on his yes, in its own sitting.

**2. The stills walkthrough: a live rendered walkthrough, not prose routes.** One axis per round (the medium, the palette, the line, the shape language, the staging of the hero moment, in whatever order the board says matters most), **three options side by side with the scene held roughly constant**, the room's recommendation and one question. Sean leans; the next round narrows; he may reach back and mix an earlier concept in; a round that goes flat changes axis rather than re-rendering. Two hosts:

- **ChatGPT desktop, the default for stills.** The room writes the opening prompt from [chatgpt-opening-prompt.md](chatgpt-opening-prompt.md) (the brief so far, the hero frame in words, the round rules) and Sean runs the rounds there on the subscription. The **capture-back ritual** closes every round before the next axis opens: the chosen frame and its prompt to `Planning/<slug>/look/`, Sean's words pasted into the sidecar verbatim and locked. No lock, no next axis.
- **Claude-native stills, for a quick in-room check.** The Codex image path (`codex exec` with image generation, as the Director and Art reach it; mc-setup-003) renders one or three frames when the room needs to see something before the next question and switching hosts would cost the evening's thread. Same ceiling (none: subscription), same capture rule, labelled generated.

**3. Motion rounds, when the piece needs one.** Only when the signature move's timing feel cannot be judged from stills (a smear, a tumble, a pull-back). Claude hosts them on Higgsfield under the ceiling, to [motion-round-form.md](motion-round-form.md): Seedance 2.5 by default, Seedance 2.0 Fast for a cheaper read, three clips with one variable changed, the meter said every round, every clip saved to `Planning/<slug>/motion/` with its prompt. A clip is a taste reference for the Animator and the motion gate, never a moving test and never authored craft.

**What the look stage leaves:** the board, the chosen hero frame and any key-shot frames by path and hash, the negatives, and Sean's words on each. **What it does not leave:** a style frame, separated layers, a palette file, a pose plan. Those are Art's.

## The brief lines the gates attack

So the room writes lines the gates can hold, the map from the ten points to [the gate schedule's attack lists](../templates/gate-schedule.md#attack-checklists-per-artifact):

| The gate reads | The attack line | So the brief-draft writes |
|---|---|---|
| Story gate | "Intake that leaked" (a brief line mapped to nothing) | one thing per ASK line, nothing decorative, each traced to a lock |
| Story gate | "The opening redesigned", "Timing squeezed" | the hero moment and signature move with their timing feel; the length as a number the channel fixed |
| Story gate | "Words shipped as final", "No room for sound" | every word Sean's or `WP<n>`; the sound scope stated |
| Story gate | "The world as two places", "Not buildable" | staging constraints the film cannot break, as non-negotiables |
| Appearance gate | "The rejected look back" | the negatives table and *What this is NOT*, naming the rejected looks by approvals id |
| Appearance gate | "An approval carried across a change" | every approved element by id, used unchanged |
| Appearance gate | "Identity judged from memory" | each subject's one design-bearing specific and what must stay recognisable |
| Appearance gate | "A target passed as a design" | the hero frame cited as a taste reference, never as the style frame |
| Motion gate | the held checks, "Felt weight and snap at speed are Sean's" | the signature move's timing feel in Sean's words, the glossary term beside it |

## The toolbelt

Named here so the room reaches them by name and nothing else; a tool the first run shows it needs is added here with Sean's yes. Skills in `anima/` are not on this machine's skill path for a studios session until linked; the first run links the three it needs under `~/.claude/skills/` and records the links in the ledger's `setup/`.

| Skill | For | Lives at |
|---|---|---|
| `creative-director` | the interview rubric behind the ten points; critique language for the rounds | `~/.claude/skills/creative-director` |
| `prompt-how-much` | how much to prompt per surface: the single lever per round, a named style token, terse imperatives for motion | `anima/.claude/skills/prompt-how-much` |
| `image-generator-prompt-science` | the construction of a still prompt when a round needs more than one lever | `anima/.claude/skills/image-generator-prompt-science` |
| `motion-art-direction` | the motion language, tone and energy, restraint, cross-shot consistency, when the grill reaches point 6 | `~/.claude/skills/motion-art-direction` |
| `animation-vocabulary` | the field's words, read beside [the glossary](../lanes/glossary.md), which wins | `~/.claude/skills/animation-vocabulary` |
| `watch` | breaking a YouTube reference down into a board row: frames, timing, what is borrowed | the `claude-video` plugin |
| `higgsfield-generate` | the motion rounds, through the `higgsfield` CLI, under the ceiling | `anima/.claude/skills/higgsfield-generate` |
| `grilling` | on request only, when Sean wants a lock stress-tested harder than the room's one push-back | `~/.claude/skills/grilling` |

**Kept out, as the seats' work:** `script-writing`, `storytelling-architecture`, `2d-animation-principles`, `screenwriting-modes`. A room that reaches for one is writing the Director's packet.
