# Lane manifest — Visual Story Director

Mandatory first read for the [Visual Story Director](../bench/visual-story-director.md). Entries are title + pointer + one-line when-to-read — shelf labels, never the books: the content lives in the private corpus (`../corpus/`, gitignored), so pointers resolve only on a machine that has it (`manifest-only` grounding otherwise, per the seat preamble). This shelf is about a film that reads before anything moves: the beat as the smallest change, one idea per shot with a known place for the eye, space that stays readable across cuts, and pace decided in a timed animatic with scratch sound. ## Vocabulary

## Principles, in learning order

Learned after the rows above and before the guides. "Named, not read" means the canon title was not read and no copy is ingested.

1. **Each beat is a change, and beats come before boards.** Canon: Blazer, *Animated Storytelling* (named, not read): grow a short from one core idea through beats and boards to an animatic. Carried by [`animatic-and-staging.md`](../corpus/guides/animatic-and-staging.md).
2. **The picture carries the meaning with the sound off.** The muted copy of the animatic proves it. Carried by the same guide.
3. **One idea per shot, with a known place for the eye.** Canon: Block, *The Visual Story* (named, not read): contrast between shapes, tones and movements raises intensity; likeness calms it. Carried by the same guide; staging is shared with Art.
4. **Space stays readable: shot size chosen per beat, screen direction held across cuts.** Carried by the staging guide's shot-size and camera sections and the rows *Shot size*, *Screen direction* and *Camera move*, until "Shot size, direction, camera moves" is written.
5. **Pace is decided in the animatic, at full length, with scratch sound.** Carried by the same guide: holds timed in frames, the whole reel watched before anything animates.

## Audit duty: the appearance packet

This seat audits Art's packet against the boards' staging, before the appearance gate (stake: *does this look stage the boards?*). Lean on the staging guide and the smear guide above, and look for what the curriculum brief says a reviewer should see in an appearance packet:

- every key pose reads as a solid black shape;
- a line of action is drawn over each key;
- squash and stretch keys keep about the resting size;
- the smear plan lists each smear's frames, the keys it joins, and its arc;
- line, pigment and paper arrive as separate layers, each with its change rule;
- a greyscale value study finds the focal point the board asks for.

A craft fail is pinned to a panel or a frame in these rows, copied from [appearance.md](appearance.md), the primary lane with the sources (*Staging* and *Layout*, which Art shares, are above):

| Term | Plain definition | Field term and synonyms |
|---|---|---|
| Pose | The character at a key moment; breakdowns show how a move travels between keys. | Key, extreme, breakdown; golden pose. |
| Line of action | The one imaginary curve through a pose that carries its force. | Not path of action, the route a move takes. |
| Silhouette | The pose filled solid; a good one reads with no inside detail. | The solid-black test. |
| Volume | How much shape there is, kept the same however it squashes or stretches. | Constant volume; losing it is off model. |
| Smear | A designed in-between that stretches the shape along a fast move, so the eye reads speed. | Smear frame; elongated in-between. |
| Multiple | One drawing showing several copies of a part, for a fast repeated move. | Multiples; flurry effect. |
| Style frame | One finished still that fixes the look before anything moves. | Colour script, for a whole film. |
| Value and temperature | Lightness from light to dark, and warm against cool, used to separate shapes. | Tone; value massing. |
| Pigment variation | Changes of colour and density inside painted areas. | Fill variation; Meier's "jittered" strokes. |
| Paper grain | The texture of the painting surface itself. | Paper texture; Paperman's paper boil. |
| Squash and stretch | Shape changing under force with volume kept, which shows what a thing is made of. | Squash and stretch. |
| Line boil | A line's small wobble frame to frame, by redrawing or a filter. | Boiling lines; Blender's Noise modifier. |
| Texture continuity | Texture moving with what it belongs to, changing only as designed. | Temporal coherence. |
