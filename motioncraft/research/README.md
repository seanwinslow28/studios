# Motioncraft's shelf of good work

Real examples of excellent every kind of motion work: launch films and explainers, painted shorts, character animation, logo stings and kinetic type, each vetted for how it turned out: who made it, how it was received, and what makes it good, measured rather than admired. Step 2 of every job starts here, then adds the examples that job needs.

One file per example or small group, holding:

- What it is, who made it, when, and a link.
- How it turned out: how it was received, and why that's believable.
- What makes it good, measured: the numbers, structure and choices behind the effect.
- What Sean said about it, in his words, if he has seen it.

Links and words only. Other people's work never goes in this repo or in a finished piece.

## Where the shelf stands

Sean loves every piece on this shelf (November 8, 2026), and each one is a target to come back to. The first focus is The Line's Freeview Play film: animated storytelling, motion, character design and a product launch in one.
