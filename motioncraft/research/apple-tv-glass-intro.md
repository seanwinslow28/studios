# Apple TV's glass logo intro (2025)

## What it is

The five-second logo sting that plays before every Apple TV show. A glass Apple logo and the letters "tv" slide through each other in layers of colour, then the camera pulls back and the logo settles, small and still, in the middle of a black screen. A short musical chord lands as it settles.

## Who made it, when, and where to watch

- **Made by:** Media Arts Lab, Apple's long-time ad agency (full name under More details), for Apple. The glass was made and filmed by Optical Arts, a London studio. The sound is by the musician Finneas.
- **When:** Released 3 November 2025, when "Apple TV+" dropped the "+" and became "Apple TV".
- **Versions:** Three lengths: a one-second sting for trailers, the five-second version before episodes, and a 12-second version before Apple's films. Finneas wrote a matching sound for each.
- **Watch:** [Apple TV's own upload of the five-second version](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6sX_BfDnyrg) (checked, plays).
- **Best moment:** 0:01.5 to 0:02.0. The coloured glass layers collapse into the finished logo in about a quarter of a second, and the chord hits a beat later.

## How it turned out

- **Won the Grand Prix for Design at Cannes Lions 2026**, the top design prize at the biggest advertising awards. ([Contagious](https://www.contagious.com/article/cannes-lions-2026-design-winners-89077875a5ac43a7b3639bb95b9702ed), [LBB](https://lbbonline.com/news/apple-cannes-lions-grand-prix-win))
- **The making-of became the story.** Within days, the design and tech press ran pieces on how it was shot with real glass, not computer graphics. ([MacRumors](https://www.macrumors.com/2025/11/06/new-apple-tv-intro-made-with-practical-effects/), [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2025/11/06/apple-tvs-colorful-new-intro-was-created-with-real-glass-and-practical-effects/), [Creative Bloq](https://www.creativebloq.com/design/logos-icons/making-of-video-shows-the-new-apple-tv-logo-is-a-real-glass-act), [PetaPixel](https://petapixel.com/2025/11/19/apple-tvs-new-logo-animation-was-handmade-by-human-creatives))
- **Why that's believable:** a Grand Prix is judged by an independent jury of designers, and the coverage came from many separate outlets. The Apple TV upload had about 400,000 views when checked.

## What makes it good, measured

I downloaded Apple's upload and measured it frame by frame (23.98 frames a second). Credits, versions and the shoot method come from the makers' accounts in the press.

- **Length:** 5.0 seconds, one unbroken shot. No cuts.
- **Two beats only:** a fast move (0 to 1.5 s), then a long settle (1.5 to 5.0 s). That's the whole piece.
- **The move:** the camera starts tight on the glass and pulls back while the layers slide sideways and stack. Motion is high and fairly even for the first 1.5 seconds.
- **The settle (this is the part Sean will like):** between 1.5 and 1.75 seconds the amount of movement drops about tenfold. It then keeps halving every few frames until about 3.0 seconds. It doesn't stop dead; it drifts to rest. From 3.0 to 5.0 seconds the picture is completely still.
- **How long the logo holds readable:** from about 1.75 seconds to the end, so about 3.25 seconds, or about two-thirds of the sting. The last 2 seconds are dead still.
- **How the sound lands:** a low swell rises from silence for the first 1.9 seconds. The main chord hits at 2.0 seconds, about a quarter-second after the logo becomes readable. That's not on the move, but just after it, as a full stop. The chord then rings out and fades to silence right at 5.0 seconds.
- **Palette:** black ground. During the move the glass throws strong magenta, blue, red, orange and green. At rest the logo turns soft and pearly: pale pink, mint and lilac on white.
- **Type:** the "tv" is set in SF TV, a version of Apple's own typeface made for this rebrand (per 9to5Mac). So it's custom.
- **What it doesn't do:** no bounce, no overshoot, no sparkle. All the colour and depth come from real light passing through real glass.

## How it was made

From the makers' accounts in the press:

- Optical Arts made large glass versions of the Apple logo and the "tv" letters, mounted them in a studio, and filmed them under carefully planned lighting.
- In the reports, the camera moves and the glass stays put (per AppleInsider's account of the making-of).
- Everything was captured in camera, then fine-tuned in post-production. ([It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/articles/optical-arts-advertisements-and-idents-film-animation-project-020926))
- Finneas told Variety he thought of classic TV sounds such as NBC's chimes and HBO's static while writing it. He wanted it to work even when you hear it ten times in a day of binge-watching. ([Variety](https://variety.com/2025/music/news/finneas-discusses-apple-tv-originals-mnemonic-intro-1236568433/); I read this through press summaries, not the full article)
