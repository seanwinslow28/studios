## Three answers carried in from old issues

- **Higgsfield on credits:** still the best hub for AI image and video. A free price check before each take, cheaper models for rough versions and the best ones for finals, and an ask before any batch over about 300 credits. Sean's account has auto-refill on; that's his setting.
- **Blender on the Mac's graphics chip:** its frames differ from the slower path by tiny amounts, which is normal. Finished renders can use the graphics chip, keeping one device, a fixed seed and one Blender version per film.
- **Locked folders:** Motioncraft doesn't keep locked or frozen folders, so Drive unlocking them doesn't matter. Numbered versions and git name tags are enough.

## Workflows to fold in

Sean sent two videos to fold into how Motioncraft works with image and video generation. Both are by the animator Simay Ezer Pekcan, made for the Flick channel and sponsored by Flick, in October 2026:

- "Making AI Animation with a Traditional Animator's Mindset": https://youtu.be/zakFNEOPq0M
- "How to Make AI Animation That Doesn't Look Generic": https://youtu.be/Bf_ZGWc5l1o

Her method, in our words, for the Higgsfield guide to build on. Higgsfield's own 3D scene builder can stand in for Flick's grey-box stage, which still needs a test.

1. **One written intent and a mood board** before any image.
2. **One fixed style description**, written once from the references and pasted word for word into every image and video prompt.
3. **Characters designed by silhouette first**, with front, side and back sheets. A new sheet whenever a pairing or prop changes. Tricky props get their own stills before any video.
4. **Each key frame from two references:** a layout image sets the framing and the style reference sets the look.
5. **Grey-box blocking:** the set roughed out in plain 3D boxes and the camera move recorded there. The video model copies the camera from it, never the look.
6. **The acting written as beats by the second**, with every reference given one labelled job in the prompt.
7. **Cheap drafts first, then finals:** Seedance drafts at low resolution cost a small fraction of a full-quality take.
8. **Cuts hidden behind something passing in front of the camera**, so several clips play as one long move.

Gaps: neither video covers sound, retake counts or the editing program. Her painted look also forgives more than a miniature-set look.
