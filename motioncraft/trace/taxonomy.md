# taxonomy — rung 1 of the ladder, Motioncraft

The tracked vocabulary a Motioncraft engagement's `trace/labels.md` draws its `failure_code` column from. This is Motioncraft's own file, for the seat failure modes its own labels earn. The families every studio shares (today the **process-waste** family) live in the shared kit's [craftwork/trace/taxonomy.md](../../craftwork/trace/taxonomy.md), and `check.py` reads both ([studio.py](studio.py) names this file). Shapes of failure only: no engagement content, no private text, nothing a recruiter should not read.

**Rung 1 has not opened for Motioncraft.** The file was created at scaffold on 2026-10-17. A seat failure mode lands here only from about thirty labelled passes of Motioncraft's own, proposed with its counts and ratified by Sean with a date. Other studios' modes are not inherited.

Until then a label carries a shared process-waste code or leaves the column blank. A blank column is the normal state, and it passes.

## Sighted, not coded

Shapes seen in a fail's critique, kept here so later labels can confirm or dissolve them. A shape earns a code only when it leads fails across Motioncraft's own labels, proposed with its counts and ratified by Sean.

- **Evidence without its frames** — an evidence item the brief asked for is made, but not on the frames it is meant to show: a motion path plotted on a blank plate instead of over a stack of the rendered frames. First sighted 2026-10-20 on the bouncing-ball exercise, the Animator's draft, the lead shape of its fail; the motion itself met every measured criterion. "Floaty weight", the shape earlier tests predicted, was not seen.
- **Record short of the contract** — the work meets every measured target, but a record the version contract requires is left out (the version's parts list), and a miss on one delivered file is written up for the reviewer instead of fixed before sealing. First sighted 2026-10-20 on the finishing exercise, Finishing's draft, the lead shape of its fail; its repair then claimed reading it had not done. A cousin of "evidence without its frames": in both, the work held and its proof fell short.
- **Record unlike its files** — the work meets its criteria, but its record states a property of a file it hands on that the file itself does not have: a layer called opaque whose exported image carries its opacity in the alpha, a range narrower than the script that made it. First sighted 2026-10-20 on the painted-layer exercise, Art's draft, the lead shape of its fail; the reviewer found it by measuring the file, not by reading the record. The third cousin: in all three, the work held and what was written about it fell short. Seen again 2026-10-21 on mc-eng-001, the first film, as the lead shape of five of its twelve fails: a cited checksum mistyped by one character when a table was rewritten, a frame count one short of the drawings it described, and three repairs whose records claimed what the repair pass itself had not done (full grounding with no corpus read; a split re-using its parent's id). The last kind recurs across E6, E7 and the film, and traces partly to the kit not carrying a draft's reads along its repair chain. With the film, the three cousins lead most of Motioncraft's labelled fails so far; whether they earn one code is for a later proposal with its counts. "Floaty weight" was again not seen. Seen again 2026-10-21 on E8, the title-card exercise, as the lead shape of its one fail: Finishing's repair declared full grounding when the pass opened no corpus file itself, its draft having done the reading.
