# Productcraft's shelf of good work

Real examples of excellent product work, from deciding whether an idea is worth pursuing to strategy, discovery and growing as a product leader, each vetted for how it turned out: who made it, how it was received, and what makes it good, measured rather than admired. Step 2 of every job starts here, then adds the examples that job needs.

One file per example or small group, holding:

- What it is, who made it, when, and a link.
- How it turned out: how it was received, and why that's believable.
- What makes it good, measured: the numbers, structure and choices behind the effect.
- What Sean said about it, in his words, if he has seen it.

Links and words only. Other people's work never goes in this repo or in a finished piece.

## Where the shelf stands

Sean saw all 9 pieces on November 12, 2026, and picked Jason Cohen's "ten people who'll pay" rule as closest to what Productcraft should do. It's the focus for now. He didn't single out the others; ask about them when a job leans on one.
