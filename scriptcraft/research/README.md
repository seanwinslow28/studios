# Scriptcraft's shelf of good work

Real examples of excellent scripts (short films, animated comedy shorts, launch-film voiceovers and sketches), each vetted for how it turned out: who made it, how it was received, and what makes it good, measured rather than admired. Step 2 of every job starts here, then adds the examples that job needs.

One file per example or small group, holding:

- What it is, who made it, when, and a link.
- How it turned out: how it was received, and why that's believable.
- What makes it good, measured: the numbers, structure and choices behind the effect.
- What Sean said about it, in his words, if he has seen it.

Links and words only. Other people's work never goes in this repo or in a finished piece.

## Sean's own scripts come first

His screenplays from before AI are the biggest sample of his voice there is, so the shelf starts from them: what he did well, and what he'd fix now. They and their readout stay private and never land in this repo. `scriptcraft/folders.local`, which git ignores, says where they are on this Mac.
