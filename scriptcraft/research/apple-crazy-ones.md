# Apple, "Here's to the Crazy Ones" (1997)

## What it is

A 60-second TV spot from the ad agency Chiat/Day: black-and-white footage of Einstein, Dylan, King, Earhart, Henson and others, under Richard Dreyfuss's voiceover. Rob Siltanen and Ken Segall are credited with the words, with Lee Clow overseeing; the credit is disputed. Steve Jobs recorded a version and chose Dreyfuss's instead. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Think_different

**Best part to look at:** the turn, "because they change things," about two-thirds through.

## How it turned out

- **The 1998 Emmy for Outstanding Commercial** (TV Academy). https://televisionacademy.com/awards/nominees-winners/1998/outstanding-commercial
- **The 2000 Grand Effie,** an award for advertising that worked, given to the whole campaign.
- **Thin spot:** Apple's comeback also had the iMac behind it, so there's no clean sales figure for this spot, and no official Apple upload to link.

## What makes it good, measured

- **Slow on purpose:** about 100 words in 60 seconds, under 2 a second, half Old Spice's pace. The pauses carry it.
- **Six beats:** a list of labels, what they do, three verbs, the turn, the reversal ("we see genius"), and a closing line that works like a proof.
- **The product is never named.** The screen shows other people's faces, then a white logo and "Think different."

### What a weaker version would have done

Mentioned the Mac, named a feature, or let the CEO narrate, which turns a statement of values into a pitch.

### Why it's here

The moving end of launch-film voiceover: a hundred words, no product, and every line earning its pause.
