# Bob's Burgers, "Best Burger" (2014)

## What it is

Season 5, episode 5, written by Mike Benner and directed by Don MacKinnon. Aired on Fox November 30, 2014, about 21 minutes. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_Burger

**Best part to look at:** the moment the two stories meet at the deadline.

## How it turned out

- **Strong reviews, not awards:** Paste gave it 9.1 out of 10 and The A.V. Club a B+. https://www.pastemagazine.com/tv/bobs-burgers/bobs-burgers-review-best-burger
- **Thin spot:** the show's Emmy winners are other episodes. This one is here because it's the cooking-competition episode.

## What makes it good, measured

- **Two stories, one deadline.** Bob competes in a burger contest; the kids race across town for the black garlic Gene forgot.
- **They meet in time,** and Bob still loses. The win is father and son, and a full restaurant after.
- **Its known weakness:** the A.V. Club's one complaint was that cutaways slow it and Gene's story starts late.

### What a weaker version would have done

Let Bob win the trophy.

### Why it's here

Sean's Go Fish, Bob is the spec he thinks caught this show best, and his cooking-competition pilot shares this episode's world. A competition story is stronger when the trophy goes to someone else.

And, November 13, 2026: "probably my favorite adult animated cartoon," for its dry, witty joke writing "filled with puns." He also named its ugly characters as part of the comedy.
