# Anthony Bourdain, Parts Unknown, "Southern Italy" (2017)

## What it is

Season 10 of CNN's Parts Unknown, aired November 26, 2017, about 43 minutes. Bourdain travels the heel of Italy and wrote his own narration. https://tv.apple.com/us/episode/umc.cmc.1hs1rehufbg2x2g30ps1jrd29

**Best part to look at:** the sea-urchin pasta, where the narration drifts from the plate into a memory.

## How it turned out

- **The 2018 Emmy for Outstanding Writing for a Nonfiction Program,** with Bourdain credited as writer (TV Academy). https://www.televisionacademy.com/awards/nominees-winners/2018/outstanding-writing-for-nonfiction-programming
- **Not a one-off:** his "Kenya" episode won the same award in 2019.
- **Thin spot:** no transcript was found, so there's no word count yet.

## What makes it good, measured

- **The narration steps back from the food** into a memory, instead of describing the taste.
- **Short punches where others use adjectives.** A beat can end on a single blunt word.
- **Not measured yet:** words per minute and how much is narration against picture; a job that needs it can time a scene.

### What a weaker version would have done

A host on camera saying "incredible" and "so fresh," the history explained, and a moral to close.

### Why it's here

Sean's Food Traveler came from his love of Bourdain's shows. This is the award-winning case of a writer's own voice carrying a food-and-travel piece, the same muscle as a voiceover.

## What Sean said about it

He named Bourdain's shows as the root of Food Traveler, November 13, 2026: built on "my obsession with Anthony Bourdain's shows."
