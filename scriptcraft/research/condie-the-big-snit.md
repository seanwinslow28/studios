# Richard Condie, The Big Snit (1985)

## What it is

A 10-minute animated short written, directed and animated by Richard Condie for the National Film Board of Canada. https://www.nfb.ca/film/big_snit/

**Best part to look at:** the moment the cat knocks out the TV just before the husband sees the war announced.

## How it turned out

- **Nominated for the Oscar** at the 58th Academy Awards, and listed on the Film Board's own Oscars page. https://nfb.ca/oscars
- **17 international awards,** including the Montreal World Film Festival's Grand Prize (Canada's TIFF film encyclopedia). https://cfe.tiff.net/content/films/big-snit
- **Number 25 in a 1994 poll of animation professionals** on the 50 greatest cartoons.

## What makes it good, measured

- **Two ladders climb side by side.** A couple's Scrabble squabble gets pettier on screen while a nuclear war breaks out that we only hear. The joke is the gap between them.
- **Where it turns:** the war is announced about a third in; the couple make up a little past halfway; from about 70% it is nearly wordless as they step out, together and unaware, into the glowing aftermath.
- **About 280 words,** many of them the newscaster's. (Timings are from an unofficial copy of the same length.)

### What a weaker version would have done

Let the couple find out, turning it into a message about nuclear war, or cut away to show the war.

### Why it's here

A small, real domestic irritation pushed up against the end of the world, with warmth underneath.
