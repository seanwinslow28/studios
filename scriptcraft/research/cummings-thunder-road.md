# Jim Cummings, Thunder Road (2016 short)

## What it is

A 13-minute short in one continuous take, written and directed by Jim Cummings, who stars. A police officer gives his mother's eulogy, and it goes wrong in real time. Made for about $7,000. https://vimeo.com/174957219

**Best part to look at:** the children's boombox failing, which forces him to perform the song himself.

## How it turned out

- **The Sundance 2016 Short Film Grand Jury Prize,** chosen from 8,712 submissions (Sundance's own announcement). https://www.sundance.org/blogs/sundance-institute-announces-short-film-awards-for-2016-sundance-film-festival-3/
- **Short of the Week's short of the year,** and a feature version that won at South by Southwest in 2018.

## What makes it good, measured

- **Something real, escalated into a public spectacle.** About half the film is a rambling eulogy with eight separate apologies; at 52% he announces her favorite song; the boombox fails; from 59% to 80% he sings and dances it with no music.
- **It ends quiet:** the last minute is wordless, with him recovering at the back of the church.
- **The most dialogue-heavy pick on the shelf:** about 1,500 words.

### What a weaker version would have done

Cut away from the dance, or let the music play. The failed boombox is what forces the spectacle.

### Why it's here

A real grief that escalates into an absurd performance, personal underneath. It runs the other way from Sean's usual: a dark surface that turns funny.
