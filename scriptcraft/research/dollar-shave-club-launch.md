# Dollar Shave Club's launch video (2012)

## What it is

"Our Blades Are F***ing Great," 93 seconds, posted to YouTube in March 2012. The founder, Michael Dubin, wrote it and stars in it, walking through his warehouse pitching $1-a-month razors. Lucia Aniello, a comedy director, cut his four-page draft down and tied each joke to a sales point. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZUG9qYTJMsI

**Best part to look at:** the first 10 seconds, where the expected "yes" gets a flat "No."

## How it turned out

- **Made for about $4,500,** with about 12,000 sign-ups in its first 48 hours and about 4.75 million views in three months, per NBC News (October 2012). https://www.nbcnews.com/news/amp/wbna49428347
- **Unilever bought the company for $1 billion in 2016** (Fortune). https://fortune.com/2016/07/21/billion-dollar-shave/
- **Thin spot:** the budget and sign-up figures are the company's own, repeated by the press. The sale is solid, but the video was one cause among many.

## What makes it good, measured

- **Every joke also sells.** Blade specs, the price, and why you don't need ten blades each arrive as a gag. A laugh lands about every 4 to 6 seconds.
- **The picture does half the work.** A toddler shaving, a machete, a man in a bear suit; the words never describe them, so the joke is the deadpan pitch against the chaos behind it.
- **Fast:** roughly 250 words in 93 seconds, about 2.7 a second (an estimate, not checked against captions).
- **The cut made it.** Four pages became 93 seconds.

### What a weaker version would have done

A founder at a desk with a price chart, or the same jokes cut loose from the product, so you laugh and forget what's being sold.

### Why it's here

The model for a funny launch film on a small budget: a founder's voice, every joke doing a sales job.

## What Sean said about it

He loves it for the same reason as Old Spice, November 13, 2026: "This is more in your face while talking about the product and doing silly things along the way with some great cut scenes." Like Old Spice, it "feels like an Adult Swim sketch."
