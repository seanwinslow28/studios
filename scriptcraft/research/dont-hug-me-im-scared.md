# Don't Hug Me I'm Scared, episode 1 (2011)

## What it is

A 3-minute puppet short by Becky Sloan and Joseph Pelling, made in their spare time with no budget. A talking sketchbook teaches three puppets about creativity, in song. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9C_HReR_McQ

**Best part to look at:** the hard snap from the gory montage back to the tidy kitchen table.

## How it turned out

- **About 85 million views** on the official upload, read off the page on November 13, 2026.
- **It led to a Channel 4 commission and later a TV series.** https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_Hug_Me_I%27m_Scared
- **Thin spot:** its festival record is less certain; a win at South by Southwest 2012 is reported but not confirmed.

## What makes it good, measured

- **Cheerful, then one burst.** A run of small "that's not creative" put-downs, then about 20 seconds of frantic horror with raw meat at 81% of the runtime, then straight back to normal.
- **The button is a straight face:** the sketchbook tells them never to be creative again.
- **Nearly all the words come before the horror:** about 160, then one straight-faced line at the end. The horror itself is purely visual.

### What a weaker version would have done

Hinted at the darkness from the start, or stayed in the horror to the end. The snap back to cheerful is what makes it.

### Why it's here

Almost exactly Sean's Petey Possum register: a cheerful kids' format with one violent burst.

## What Sean said about it

He loves it, November 13, 2026: "a cross between a familiar childhood kids' show" turned into "a surrealist musical comedy-horror with dark turns. It stays funny because they're puppets."
