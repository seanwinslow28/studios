# Mulan: the training song (1998)

## What it is

The training montage from Disney's Mulan, set to one song, about three and three-quarter minutes. A camp of hopeless recruits becomes an army, and the smallest of them works out how to fetch an arrow from the top of a pole. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vGfJeW_CcFY

**Best part to look at:** 2:26 to 3:19. The low point, the look at the weights, and the climb.

## How it turned out

- **The film took $304 million worldwide** and sits at 91% on Rotten Tomatoes (Wikipedia's pages for the film and the song).
- **Empire ranked this 14th among cinema's training montages** (same pages).

## What makes it good, measured

Measured from the Disney upload on November 15, 2026, from frames. Times are the clip's. The clip is small and cropped, so cinema timings may run about 4% longer. Nobody listened.

- **112 shots, 2.0 seconds on average.** The failing stretch runs 1.8 seconds a shot, the low point 2.9, the mastery run 1.4, and its last ten shots 1.0.
- **Fail long, master short.** Eleven skills. The failures average about 12 seconds each. Ten of the eleven come back mastered at about 2 seconds each, nine of them in one 22-second run.
- **One planted challenge, paid off by an idea.** The arrow is shown in the pole at 0:20, and again at the low point. It lands at the captain's feet 177 seconds later. What changes is not strength: she loops the weights' straps round the pole and walks up.
- **The jokes stop at the low point.** A gag about every 7 seconds until 2:41. None after. In their place, one reversal: the captain floored, and smiling.
- **The low point is the slowest part.** 37 seconds, with the longest shot in the sequence, 7.6 seconds, ending on her alone, eyes down, then up. Nobody helps her.
- **How she is picked out:** the smallest shape, a green sash in every costume, last and alone in each skill, the only recruit given close-ups.
- **The last picture** holds 2.5 seconds: she is in the air, highest in the frame, staff in hand.

### What a weaker version would have done

Shown every skill improving a little at a time. Here they are bad for a long while, and then good all at once.

### Why it's here

The model for a one-song training stretch: what to plant, where the jokes stop, how short mastery can be.
