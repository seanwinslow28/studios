# Old Spice, "The Man Your Man Could Smell Like" (2010)

## What it is

A 33-second TV spot, online February 4, 2010. Written by Craig Allen and Eric Kallman at Wieden+Kennedy, directed by Tom Kuntz, starring Isaiah Mustafa. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=owGykVbfgUE

**Best part to look at:** the whole thing in one take, as the set changes under his words.

## How it turned out

- **The 2010 Emmy for Outstanding Commercial** (TV Academy). https://www.televisionacademy.com/awards/nominees-winners/2010/outstanding-commercial
- **The Cannes Lions Film Grand Prix,** June 2010.
- **Sales:** body-wash sales up 60% by May against a 15% goal, per the brand and agency in Muse by Clio's oral history. https://musebyclios.com/advertising/behind-the-towel-an-oral-history-of-the-legendary-old-spice-ad/
- **Thin spot:** the sales figures are the brand's own, and the often-quoted "107%" was one month of a later campaign.

## What makes it good, measured

- **The picture obeys the voiceover.** Bathroom, boat, oyster, tickets, diamonds, horse: about six changes in 33 seconds, one every five or so. The writers called it almost a radio script.
- **A rhythm before the surprise.** The "look at your man, now back to me" pattern repeats before the set starts moving.
- **Very fast:** about 108 words in 33 seconds, over 3 a second (a count from memory, not captions).
- **The button comes after the sell:** "I'm on a horse," a reveal that explains nothing.
- **The insight was the audience:** it talks to the women who buy most men's body wash.

### What a weaker version would have done

Told men they'd smell good, listed the features, and ended on a product shot with a tagline.

### Why it's here

The funniest 30-second voiceover there is, and proof that a launch film's words can drive every picture.

## What Sean said about it

He loves it, November 13, 2026: "it introduced absurdity and Adult Swim type comedy into the world of advertisements. It's possible others did that too (beer commercials, I'm sure), but Old Spice stands out to me." It "feels like an Adult Swim sketch, which is great."
