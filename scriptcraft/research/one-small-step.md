# One Small Step (2018)

## What it is

A seven-minute animated short with no dialogue, by Taiko Studios, directed by Andrew Chesworth and Bobby Pontillas. A girl who wants to be an astronaut grows up above her father's shoe-repair shop. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yYcpRSQ-irs

**Best part to look at:** 3:49 to 5:44. His death, told with an empty kitchen, then the box of her old shoes.

## How it turned out

- **Nominated for the Oscar for animated short** at the 91st Academy Awards (NBC News, https://www.nbcnews.com/news/asian-america/animated-short-film-one-small-step-celebrates-pursuit-dream-n974321).
- **39 festival wins** on the studio's own list (https://www.taikostudios.com/awards.html). That tally is the studio's claim.
- **Thin spot:** the nomination is public record; the rest rests on the studio.

## What makes it good, measured

Measured from the official upload on November 15, 2026. The film runs 0:07 to 7:10. Timed from frames; nobody listened, so the notes on music rest on pictures of the sound.

- **Who she is and what she wants, in ten seconds.** At 0:17, one picture: a small girl in a toy space helmet, lit by a TV, a toy rocket beside her.
- **No spoken words found.** The story rides on shoes on one rack, his sewing machine, letters on a doormat, and one side-on kitchen picture used 12 times.
- **The death takes 42 seconds and four shots, and never shows him.** His empty chair, his cup, his cane on the floor (3:49). She climbs the stairs out of the picture (4:02). A hard cut to black for 3.5 seconds. A headstone. No music from 3:20 to 5:02.
- **Every object she finds was on screen minutes earlier.** In a box by his worktable: the track shoe (set up at 2:38), the flip-flop he mended (2:18), and her childhood astronaut boot (0:41). The turn is ten seconds of her holding the boot to her chest, 5:18 to 5:28.
- **She rebuilds in the same pictures she failed in.** A 19-second run replays her failures in the same places: an F becomes an A, her fall on the track becomes helping a fallen runner.
- **Holds:** the empty kitchen 6.7 seconds, a grey street 5.3, her dark bedroom 4.7. The last picture, her smile inside the helmet, holds 4.3.
- **About 100 shots,** 4.2 seconds on average.

### What a weaker version would have done

Shown the father dying, or given her a letter from him. Here the mended shoes are the letter.

### Why it's here

The model for a loss told without a deathbed and a discovery made through objects, with no words.
