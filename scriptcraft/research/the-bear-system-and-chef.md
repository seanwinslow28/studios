# The Bear's pilot, "System" (2022), against Chef (2014)

## What it is

Two kitchens, two lessons.

- **The Bear, "System":** written and directed by Christopher Storer, 28 minutes, on Hulu June 23, 2022. https://www.fxnetworks.com/shows/the-bear
- **Chef:** written and directed by Jon Favreau, who stars; 114 minutes; opened the South by Southwest festival in 2014. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chef_(2014_film)

**Best part to look at:** The Bear's last minute, with no dialogue, against Chef's first act.

## How it turned out

- **"System" won the Emmy for Outstanding Writing for a Comedy Series,** and its editor won the Emmy for editing it. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_(The_Bear)
- **Chef cost $11 million and made about $46 to $50 million,** with 87% on Rotten Tomatoes. Several critics, Variety's among them, said its conflict drains away once the food truck hits the road.

## What makes it good, measured

- **The Bear is loud in the middle and silent at both ends.** It opens on a wordless dream and closes on Carmy throwing out a can of tomatoes meant for his dead brother's recipe. The grief is all objects and routines, never a speech.
- **Pace:** its script is reportedly 38 pages for 28 minutes, about 1.35 pages a minute against the usual one, because the dialogue overlaps. The first cut ran 39 minutes; 11 came out.
- **Chef's strong half is its first act:** the critic, the online fight, the meltdown. Once every obstacle is gone, it turns into a travel montage.

### What a weaker version would have done

The Bear would explain the brother's death in dialogue and end on a hug. Chef's weaker version is its own second half.

### Why it's here

Sean pictured Gourmet looking and feeling like The Bear before it existed, and Chef was one of its inspirations. Side by side they show that pressure keeps a story going and comfort ends it.

## What Sean said about it

He named both as touchstones for Gourmet, November 13, 2026: he pictured it like The Bear, "more slapstick/dark comedy and less stress though," and Chef "was also an inspiration."
