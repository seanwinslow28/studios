# Too Many Cooks (2014)

## What it is

An 11-minute short created by Casper Kelly for Adult Swim, first aired at 4 a.m. in an infomercial slot on October 28, 2014. Shot in three days, mostly with local extras. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QrGrOK8oZG8

**Best part to look at:** the first two minutes, where the killer is already in the background before anyone notices.

## How it turned out

- **About 30 million views** on the official upload, and over 5 million in its first week.
- **Wide press:** Rolling Stone called it an instant cult classic. https://www.inverse.com/entertainment/50167-too-many-cooks-oral-history-anniversary
- **Thin spot:** no awards, because of its format; the evidence is press and views.

## What makes it good, measured

- **One idea, pushed too far on purpose:** a 1980s sitcom title sequence that never ends. Kelly wanted it to run "uncomfortably long."
- **Six genre shifts** after the sitcom opening (crime show, soap, cartoon and on to a slasher), with the full slasher turn at about 44%.
- **The killer is planted at 20 seconds,** long before the turn.
- **Words thin out as the pictures take over:** about 120 a minute in the first three minutes, about 65 after.
- **The button:** the credits finally end and the "show" lasts seconds.

### What a weaker version would have done

A two-minute credits parody with a killer reveal at the end, or the killer shown plainly from the start with nothing to discover.

### Why it's here

A cheerful, dated TV format escalated scene by scene into horror, and very visual: Sean's "how would that escalate?" taken to its limit.
