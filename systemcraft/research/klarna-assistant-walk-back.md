# Klarna's AI assistant: big launch numbers, then a public walk-back (2024 to 2025)

## What it is

The customer-service chatbot of Klarna, a payments company, built with OpenAI and launched in early 2024. This one is on the shelf as a warning.

- Klarna's announcement, February 27, 2024: https://www.klarna.com/international/press/klarna-ai-assistant-handles-two-thirds-of-customer-service-chats-in-its-first-month/
- The walk-back, reported May 9, 2025, from an interview the chief executive gave the day before: https://www.customerexperiencedive.com/news/klarna-reinvests-human-talent-customer-service-AI-chatbot/

**Best part to look at:** the list of numbers in the announcement, beside what the chief executive said fifteen months later.

## How it turned out

- **Month one, by Klarna's count:** 2.3 million conversations, two-thirds of all chats, the work of 700 full-time agents, customers finished in under 2 minutes where it had taken 11, repeat inquiries down 25%, and a forecast $40 million gain in profit. Satisfaction was called equal to human agents, with no score given.
- **Fifteen months later** the chief executive said cost had weighed too heavily in how they judged it, and that quality had suffered. Klarna began hiring human agents again.
- **The bot was not switched off.** It reportedly still handles about two-thirds of chats. What Klarna said it would change was the route to a person: always on offer.
- **Why it's believable:** the company's own leader, on the record, against the company's own announcement.

## What it shows, measured

- **Almost every launch number was about speed, volume or cost.** The two about quality were the 25% drop in repeat inquiries and a satisfaction score called equal to humans, with no figure.
- **Nothing reported how the hard cases went.**
- **What Klarna said it would change was the design, not the model:** a person always on offer, treated as part of the product. It began with a pilot.

### How it works, in plain steps

1. A customer opens chat in the app.
2. The assistant answers: refunds, returns, payments, disputes.
3. The 2024 announcement says customers could still choose a live agent.
4. In May 2025 Klarna said it wanted a person always on offer, and began a pilot with a human team.

### What a stronger version would have done

Measured quality on the hard cases from day one, and designed the handover to a person as a real step with its own target.

### Side by side

- **Air Canada's website chatbot.** A British Columbia tribunal ruled on February 14, 2024 that the airline had to honour a refund rule its chatbot got wrong, and that a company answers for everything on its website, chatbot included. It ordered 812.02 Canadian dollars paid. Summary: https://dww.com/articles/bc-tribunal-finds-air-canada-liable-for-inaccurate-advice-given-by-website-chatbot. Klarna leaned on the wrong numbers; the tribunal found Air Canada hadn't taken reasonable care to make its chatbot accurate.
- **Honeycomb** (`honeycomb-query-assistant.md`) published the goals it missed in its own results post. Klarna's misses took fifteen months and an interview to surface.

### Why it's here

A design is judged by what it measures. This is the best-known case of an AI system whose launch numbers all looked excellent, and whose own chief executive later said quality had suffered because cost had weighed too heavily.
