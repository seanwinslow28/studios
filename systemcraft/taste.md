# Systemcraft's taste

Sean's taste for designs for AI systems, in his own words: the things he caught and wants caught next time. The lead reads this page at the start of every job, and the front door's brainstorm bends ideas through it.

How this page works:

- Each rule is one line in Sean's words, with the example he caught and when.
- About 15 rules at most. Only Sean raises that ceiling.
- A rule joins when Sean has given the same note about five times and says yes to the lead's proposal, or when he writes one himself.
- Rules come from taste, never from something that went wrong once. A mistake gets fixed in the work.

## Rules

Written with Sean on November 16, 2026, from his shelf pick, his rulings on earlier agent systems, and his notes.

1. **"It's worked as long as we provide the right tools and the right knowledge."** A system is only as good as what its agents are given. His pick of the long-job harness from the shelf (November 16).
2. **"Set up guardrails so that it doesn't do anything outside of what it's job is in that moment."** Each part has one job and stays in it. Choosing guarded agent renders for Motioncraft (October 19).
3. **"Then it will find them. Even if it has to make them up."** A checker is never told it HAS to find flaws. The Productcraft review loop (September 14).
4. **"There's absolutely no way for me to do all 34 of these."** Ask only for the time he'll really give. Grading 34 agent steps, which felt "like looking at another language" (October 21).
5. **"That's too much reading and editing."** One draft where reading is heavy, several where it's cheap. One Substack draft, several X posts (September 2).
6. **"I can't see how you'll put them together until the draft is out."** Show the shape before the work is made. The content machine's interview (late August).
7. **"Week 1 was a failure, so we made the change immediately."** Try it on real work and change it as soon as it fails. The topic-finder's trial (September 2).
8. **"If it feels off, I'll get rid of it."** Every optional part is on trial, and his sense counts. The content machine (September 5).
9. **"Me tweaking things in my codebase isn't always going to be a story."** Judge a system by whether what it hands him is worth having, not whether it ran. The topic-finder's first week (August 31).
10. **"I didn't expect every single [agent] to flip."** The bigger model only where it's needed. Making Codex the step-up, not the default (September 10).
11. **"Read-only until a tool earns write access, then pull requests only."** A new part starts able to read and nothing more. His note on working with AI.
12. **"We can't keep going back and forth for hours… The models won't stop."** Every loop has a stop, and what's left comes to him. The fitness-app review loop (September 14).
13. **"I don't want to keep going down a rabbit hole of loop holes and workarounds if we have an end result to learn from."** Start from something that works and study it. His portfolio site (August 16).
